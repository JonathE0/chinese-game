# 寻根 · Roots — story and sandbox design (draft concept)

Status: concept agreed in conversation on 2026-09-28; not yet planned or built. The Chinese
below is a first draft for illustration and needs review before it goes into the game.

## Why

The game teaches well but gives little reason to keep playing: you run around learning words with
no goal beyond exploring. A story with a hard ending would fix motivation but kill the sandbox.
This design gives the player a reason to be here and a quest that never runs out, while the world
stays open and the daily life keeps going after any milestone.

## Premise

You are a young overseas Chinese (华裔) who grew up abroad and barely speaks the language. Your
grandmother, 奶奶, grew up in 青禾. She is too old to travel now, so she sends you instead, with
her old photo album. On the back of each photo she wrote a line for you. She asks you to find
those places, meet her old friends, and send her postcards of how things look today.

This explains why you are a beginner, why the locals are patient with you, and why every street
matters. Keeping 奶奶 alive and far away keeps the tone warm: she writes back.

- Opening: you arrive in the square with the album. 林阿姨 recognises 奶奶's photo on the first
  page and becomes your first guide (she already runs the tea stall beside the old teahouse).
- The home you decorate is 奶奶's old house, which the family kept.

## Layer 1 — the album, the quest that never ends

Each photo is a small story. The loop:

1. **Read** the note on the back (reading practice, scaled to your level).
2. **Find** the place: ask people the way (问路), follow what they say.
3. **Meet** the person or place in the photo and hear its story (listening practice).
4. **Retake** the shot with the 打卡 camera. The album shows the old photo and yours side by side.
5. **Send** 奶奶 a postcard about it; her reply arrives a day later (voiced).

Example notes (draft):

| Note on the back | Pinyin | English | Where it leads |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1986年春，和老林在广场喝茶。 | 1986 nián chūn, hé Lǎo Lín zài guǎngchǎng hē chá. | Spring 1986, drinking tea with Old Lin on the square. | 林阿姨's family and the old teahouse |
| 我们的茶楼开张了！ | Wǒmen de chálóu kāizhāng le! | Our teahouse opened! | The teahouse restoration project |
| 1990年中秋，在河边看灯。 | 1990 nián Zhōngqiū, zài hébiān kàn dēng. | Mid-Autumn 1990, watching the lanterns by the river. | 河边文化街, retaken on 中秋节 night |
| 第一次坐火车去云海。 | Dì-yī cì zuò huǒchē qù Yúnhǎi. | The first time I took the train to Yunhai. | The old station is now the metro; 云海 then and now |
| 莲池公园，你爷爷在这儿向我求婚。 | Liánchí Gōngyuán, nǐ yéye zài zhèr xiàng wǒ qiúhūn. | Lotus Pond Park: your grandfather proposed to me here. | The park pavilion; a quiet, personal chapter |

Why it never runs out:

- Photos can show places that aren't built yet (a mountain village, a beach town, a train to
  another city). They sit in the album blurred, with a hint, until that region exists. The album is
  how new regions enter the game.
- Photos can be seasonal (festivals, snow, night) and only complete at the right time.
- People in the photos have their own follow-up stories, which feed friendships.

## Layer 2 — projects you choose (sandbox)

A board of long-term projects. All optional, any order, several at once:

- Restore 奶奶's teahouse (the construction site already exists); once open it becomes a business
  you keep running.
- Open your own stall at the night market.
- Rent an apartment in 云海 and decorate it.
- Part-time jobs taking orders in Chinese for coins: the noodle shop, the ferry ticket booth, the
  mall's bubble tea bar, later the teahouse.
- Earn HSK certificates (the placement test and mock exams already exist).
- Ride every ferry and metro line; fill every 打卡 stamp.

## Layer 3 — daily life and a rank that grows

- Daily errands, friends, cooking, shopping and festivals continue with no finish line; festivals
  come back every in-game year with new events.
- A rank you climb by combining language (HSK), friends, places (album and 打卡), and skills
  (cooking, tea, jobs), not by one path:

| Rank | Pinyin | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 游客 | yóukè | tourist |
| 常客 | chángkè | regular |
| 半个青禾人 | bàn ge Qīnghé rén | half a local |
| 青禾人 | Qīnghé rén | a local |
| 老青禾 | lǎo Qīnghé | an old hand |

- The town reacts as you rise: people greet you by name, shopkeepers remember what you like, a
  friend invites you to dinner, someone gives you a key. Scaffolding comes down and decorations go
  up as your projects progress.

## What it reuses

The 打卡 camera and album, 问路, friends and postcards, festivals and the moon, the teahouse
construction site and permits, the bank, shops and bargaining, cooking, the metro and ferry, the
city interiors and mall, HSK certificates and mock exams, daily errands, and the home decorating.

## Two fixes that help whatever we build

- The quest card shows the current goal with a progress bar.
- The world visibly changes as you progress.

## Open questions

- 奶奶's name, and whether you are her 孙子 or 孙女 (or keep it neutral).
- How many photos for the first release (suggested: 10–12 across the three districts and 云海).
- Whether jobs pay coins only, or also raise the relevant rank skill.
- Which region the first "not built yet" photo points to.
