===============================================================================
 青禾小镇 - Little Mandarin Town
 How to run the game on this computer
===============================================================================

The game is RUNNING RIGHT NOW. Open this in your browser:

    http://127.0.0.1:5174

That address only works on this computer. It is a local server, not a website,
so nobody else can open it and it does not work from your phone.

If that link does not load, the server has been stopped. Start it again with
the three steps in section 2.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 1. WHAT YOU NEED (one-time)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Node.js version 22.12 or newer. Check what you have by opening a terminal and
typing:

    node --version

This machine currently has v24.18.0, which is fine.

If you ever need to reinstall Node, get it from https://nodejs.org (take the
LTS build).

You do NOT need Python, Unity, or a PlayCanvas account to play. Python is only
needed if you want to regenerate the voice clips or word lists - see section 6.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 2. RUNNING THE GAME (the normal way)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

STEP 1 - Open a terminal in the project folder.

    Easiest way: open File Explorer, go to

        C:\Users\jonat\Documents\ChatGPT\Game

    click in the address bar at the top, type  powershell  and press Enter.

    (Or open PowerShell anywhere and run:
        cd C:\Users\jonat\Documents\ChatGPT\Game
    )

STEP 2 - Install the dependencies. You only ever need to do this once, or
         after the project's package.json changes.

    npm install

    This takes a minute or two the first time and creates a node_modules
    folder. There is nothing to configure.

STEP 3 - Start the game server.

    npm run dev -- --port 5174

    You will see something like:

        VITE v7.3.6  ready in 400 ms
        ->  Local:   http://127.0.0.1:5174/

STEP 4 - Open http://127.0.0.1:5174 in your browser and click 开始旅行.

Leave that terminal window open. The game runs for as long as it is open.
To stop the server, click the terminal and press Ctrl+C.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 3. PLAYING
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  Click the town once   grab the mouse so you can look around
  Mouse                 look
  W A S D / arrows      walk (relative to where you are looking)
  Shift                 run
  Space                 jump (onto benches, planters, low walls) / stand up
  F                     learn the Chinese name of whatever you are looking at
  E                     talk / enter a door / use a counter / sit down / decorate
  V                     third person (outdoors and at home only)
  H                     hide or show the name that follows the crosshair
  1 2 3 4 5             journal, bag, word bank, condition, settings
                        (ignored while you are typing or mid-conversation)
  Click / G             throw what you are holding / put it down
  Esc                   release the mouse, or close a panel

  ? buttons             reveal pinyin / English (choose which in Settings)
  Settings              also has a mouse sensitivity slider, if the view turns
                        faster or slower than you would like
  Select any Chinese    pop-up dictionary with pinyin and meaning

If the view stops turning, the mouse has been released - the dark "click the
town" banner in the middle of the screen says so. Click once and it comes back.

Tidying the screen up:
  - The chevron on the mission card folds it away; the count stays visible.
  - The eye button in the top bar (or H) hides the word in the middle.
  - The button at the right end of the key hints switches them between
    English, Chinese and both. English is the default; the Chinese is never
    removed, only hidden.
  All three choices are saved with your progress.

On a touchscreen: drag the right half of the screen to look, and hold the lower
left as a thumbstick to walk.

Tap a mission in the card and the minimap draws a dashed line to where it
happens. Tap it again to turn the line off.

Where to go first:
  - 林阿姨 at the tea stall on the left will say hello (quest 1).
  - 小美 near the north building teaches four words (quest 2).
  - 陈叔叔 on the right sells souvenirs and haggles (quest 3).
  - 家居小铺 on the west side of the square sells beds, tables, rugs and shoes
    from day one, so you can furnish your room before any district opens.
  - 青禾银行 next to it lends money once 小美 has taught you your first words.
  - 旧物铺 on the east side buys things back, once you have bought anything.
    Its price for each item is rerolled every in-game day.
  - The building marked 词语馆 is the HSK word hall - walk in and use the
    lectern.
  - The east street is gated. Learn 25 HSK level 1 words (drill in the word
    hall, or walk around pressing F) and the gate opens.

Picking things up:
  - Press E at a fruit basket, a bakery case or a supermarket rack to lift one out.
  - Click to throw it; G puts it down. It bounces, rolls and settles like a real object.
  - The stall restocks the gap by itself, and the street is tidied when you leave.
  - These are toys, not shopping: you cannot eat or sell them.

Shopping:
  - Fixed-price shops have a basket, shown at the top of the shop. Either use the
    "加入购物车" button under any item, or open an item and set a quantity there.
  - Cards show what is already in the basket. "看看清单" gives the itemised bill,
    where you can still change quantities before paying for the lot at once.
  - 陈叔叔's stall is the exception - haggling is one item at a time, and it says so.

At the bank (青禾银行, west side of the square):
  - Deposit money and it pays 2% interest every in-game morning, up to 25 a day.
    You need about 1250 saved to reach that ceiling.
  - Building permits for large projects: pay in full, or over four in-game weeks
    for a surcharge. Instalments come out automatically.
  - Loans are still there, on their own tab.

Going into the city (the metro stair on the north side of the square):
  - Press E at the stair to open the fare hall.
  - Buy a single ticket (6) or a seven-day pass (40), then press the board button.
  - The tunnel scene runs for about seven seconds; Space or Esc skips it.
  - In 云海市中心: talk to the people on the pavement, look at things and press F for
    city words the town does not have, and try the 便利店 on the west pavement.
  - To come home, walk into the station hall at the south end and press E. It is free.

Cooking at home (through the green door on the right-hand wall of your house):
  - Buy groceries at 青禾超市: noodles, eggs, rice, tofu, a tomato, some greens.
  - Walk through the kitchen door and press E at the stove.
  - Pick a recipe and press 开火. It takes 20-35 seconds; you can leave and come back.
  - Press 盛出来 when it is done, then eat it. One meal fills your hunger completely,
    for roughly half what the same amount of restaurant food would cost.

Building something (the hoarded lot north of the square):
  - Get the shop permit from the bank first.
  - Then gather timber, bricks and cloth. 家居小铺 sells all three.
  - Hand them in a few at a time; the site remembers what you have given.
  - When it is full, break ground. 青禾茶楼 goes up and pays you every morning.

At the library (青禾书馆, shopping street):
  - Books are shelved by difficulty and marked by whether you can read them yet.
  - A book that is still too hard tells you which words you are missing and can
    put six of them straight into your word bank.
  - Highlight any line while reading to see what the whole sentence means.

After dark:
  - From 18:00 two food carts are pushed into the square and trade until 02:00:
    candied hawthorn on one, skewers and tofu pudding on the other.

Looking after yourself:
  - You get hungry and tired, and both slow you down. The heart button in the
    top bar shows how you are doing.
  - Anything edible in your bag can be eaten from there or from that panel.
  - Sleep in your own bed to choose the time of day: morning, midday, evening
    or night. Sleeping fills your rest back up.
  - Shoes change how fast you walk. Being well dressed, and not owing the bank
    money, makes shopkeepers easier to haggle with.
  - The journal has three errands a day. They refresh every in-game day and
    pay coins when you collect them.
  - A poster by the door at home repeats all of this whenever you forget it.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 4. YOUR SAVE FILE
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Progress is stored in your browser, not in a file. It survives closing the
browser, but it is erased if you clear site data for 127.0.0.1, and it is
per-browser (Chrome and Edge do not share it).

Back it up: Settings (the sun icon, top right) -> 导出存档. That downloads
qinghe-save.json. Use 导入存档 to load it back.

Use one browser tab at a time. Two tabs will overwrite each other's save.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 5. RUNNING THE BUILT VERSION INSTEAD (optional)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Section 2 runs a development server, which is what you want day to day. If you
want the optimised build - what you would actually put on a website - run:

    npm run build
    npm run preview

That serves the contents of the dist/ folder, usually on

    http://127.0.0.1:4173

It starts faster and loads faster, but you have to re-run "npm run build"
after any change to the game's code or content.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 6. REGENERATING VOICES, WORD LISTS AND THE DICTIONARY (optional)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Only needed if you edit the Chinese content. The generated files are already
committed, so playing needs none of this.

These three scripts need Python 3 and an internet connection. Set up once:

    python -m venv .venv
    .venv\Scripts\python.exe -m pip install edge-tts

Then:

    .venv\Scripts\python.exe scripts\generate-voice.py

        Re-synthesises any voice clip whose text or voice changed. It skips
        everything unchanged, so it is quick unless you added a lot.

    (HSK cards: download the four sources into .cache\hsk-sources first;
     README.md, "Regenerating the built data", has the exact commands. Then:)
    .venv\Scripts\python.exe scripts\prepare-hsk-chinese.py
    .venv\Scripts\python.exe scripts\build-hsk.py .cache\hsk-sources\hsk-source.json
    .venv\Scripts\python.exe scripts\build-dictionary.py cedict.txt

        These two need their source files downloaded first. The exact download
        command is written at the top of each script.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 8. IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

"Port 5174 is already in use"
    A server is already running on it - just open http://127.0.0.1:5174 and
    play. If you want a fresh one, either use a different port:

        npm run dev -- --port 5180

    or find and stop the old one:

        netstat -ano | findstr :5174
        taskkill /PID <the number in the last column> /F

"The 3D scene could not start"
    Your browser needs WebGL2 and hardware acceleration. In Edge or Chrome go
    to Settings -> System, turn on "Use graphics acceleration when available",
    and restart the browser.

No sound
    Audio only starts after you click 开始旅行 - browsers block sound before
    you interact with the page. Check the three volume sliders in Settings,
    and that the browser tab is not muted (right-click the tab).

The mouse will not look around
    Click once on the town itself to lock the pointer. Press Esc to get the
    cursor back.

"npm is not recognised"
    Node.js is not installed or not on your PATH. Install it from
    https://nodejs.org and open a NEW terminal window.

A blank white page
    Press F12, open the Console tab, and read the first red error. Nine times
    out of ten it is a missing node_modules - run "npm install" again.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 9. THINGS TO KNOW
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The Mandarin voices are AI generated (Microsoft Edge neural TTS), not native
speaker recordings, and nobody has listened through all 2,318 of them. The game
labels them AI 配音 wherever it plays one. Do not publish the game publicly
without checking the licence terms - see docs\VOICE_PRODUCTION.md.

The HSK levels come from a public dataset and have not been checked word by
word against the official syllabus. The word hall says so on screen.

The pop-up dictionary is CC-CEDICT, licensed CC BY-SA 4.0.

Fuller documentation is in README.md.
